The not so drunk text - prints/OOH/

Maes
Personal project
2025

Why wait for a drunken buzz to say what you really mean? With Maes 0.0, you’ve got the courage (sober).

Insight

‍

Many people have sent a drunk text: unfiltered, brutally honest, straight from the gut. That’s what alcohol does: it gives us the courage to say what’s really on our minds. But here’s the question: do we really need to be drunk to be that honest?

‍

Idea

‍

That’s where Maes 0.0 comes in. I created a series of horizontal prints designed to look like surfaces for “drunk texts” to be pasted on. But imagine this: what if Maes flips the narrative? What if they encourage people to speak their truth, without needing a drink first?
